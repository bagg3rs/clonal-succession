Family

Each colour is one family of tumour cells, grown from one stem cell (ringed while it lives). Bright: dividing. Pale: stopped dividing without finishing differentiating. White, fading to grey: dying. A family never comes back: each has a limited number of divisions, and in this model the next family starts once the last has stopped dividing. That rule is invented; what really starts it is not known. The numbers are invented too, and the swings in size come from them, not from Bruce’s idea. Back to the walk-through
